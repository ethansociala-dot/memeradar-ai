Research checklistUse before forming an opinion
Liquidity and market depthCHECK

Is there enough liquidity? Could a trade meaningfully move the price? Are liquidity conditions changeable?

Holder concentrationCHECK

Are a few wallets holding a large share? Are wallets linked or controlled by the same party?

Token permissionsCHECK

Are minting, freezing, pausing, or upgrade permissions present? Has the contract been independently reviewed?

Promotion and transparencyCHECK

Are claims independently verifiable? Watch for guaranteed-return promises, urgency, undisclosed promotions, and impersonation.
